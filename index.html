How To Play

Objective

Mega Tic Tac Toe is a three-layer version of Ultimate Tic Tac Toe. The goal is to win the Mega Board. First win Small Boards. Then use those to win Ultimate Boards. Finally, win Ultimate Boards to capture the Mega Board and win the game.

Starting The Game

Every game begins in the center board of the center Ultimate Board.

Taking A Turn

Press Continue to enter the currently active Small Board. Select any empty cell to place your mark.

Small Boards

Each Small Board works exactly like normal Tic Tac Toe. Get three in a row vertically, horizontally, or diagonally to win the Small Board. Won Small Boards become permanently colored the winning player's color.

Ultimate Boards

Each Ultimate Board contains nine Small Boards. Win three Small Boards in a row inside an Ultimate Board to capture that Ultimate Board.

Mega Board

The Mega Board contains nine Ultimate Boards. Win three Ultimate Boards in a row to win the entire game.

Board Routing

Where you choose to play determines where the next player must play. Example: If you play in the upper-right corner of a small board in the center of an ultimate board, the next player must play in the upper-right small board in the center ultimate board.

Free Moves

If a move would send the next player to a completed board, a FREE MOVE is awarded. When FREE MOVE appears at the top of the screen, the player may choose any unfinished board to play in.

History Controls

The Back button steps backward through the game. The Forward button steps forward. Continue immediately returns to the current game position.

Import And Export

Individual games can be exported as save files. Those save files may later be imported onto another device.

Tips

Don't focus only on the Small Board you are currently playing. Think several moves ahead and consider where your move will force your opponent to play next. Controlling future destinations is often more important than winning a Small Board quickly.
